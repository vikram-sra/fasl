## Embedded 3D crop assets

Downloaded from 3dassets.dev on 2026-10-07; both asset pages identify the license as CC0. The provider describes these as AI-assisted, stylized low-poly models, not botanical scans.

- `models/rice-mature.glb`: [Rice (Mature)](https://3dassets.dev/assets/cozy-farm-and-homestead-rice-mature-28d8a6a5), original download https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/19389/v1/model.glb.
- `models/wheat-mature.glb`: [Wheat (Mature)](https://3dassets.dev/assets/cozy-farm-and-homestead-wheat-mature-bc511f66), original download https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/19380/v1/model.glb.

Original files are retained. Runtime removes the included soil base, dequantizes coordinates, and normalizes crop height to illustrative metre dimensions. No textures were added. GLTFLoader comes from Three.js r182 under the bundled `app/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt` license.
